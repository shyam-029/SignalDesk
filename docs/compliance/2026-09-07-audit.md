# SignalDesk Compliance & Pre-Launch Risk Audit

Audit date: 7 September 2026. Engineering review only; not legal advice.

## Implemented
- Legal routes: Privacy, Terms, Cookies, Refunds, Business/Legal Details, linked from the footer.
- Explicit acknowledgement before research-assistant submission, explaining LLM-provider processing and linking to Privacy.
- Skip navigation, main landmark, labelled research textarea, dialog semantics, Escape handling and reduced-motion support.
- Bundled fonts; no remote font request required.
- Repository review found no analytics SDK, advertising pixel, cookie-setting code, payment gateway, iframe/video/social embed, or fake testimonial/review component.
- Stock marks are generated monograms; charts are application-generated SVG/Lightweight Charts.
- Product copy states that analysis is informational/not investment advice and third-party data may be incomplete or inaccurate.

## Cookie consent
The current frontend does not set cookies or run non-essential browser tracking. Theme preference uses local storage. A cookie banner is therefore not required merely because the current site is visited. Revisit this before adding analytics, advertising, reCAPTCHA, social widgets, embedded video, behavioural tracking or authentication cookies. Where legally required, block non-essential technologies until affirmative consent and provide withdrawal/change controls.

## Form consent
There is no account, marketing, contact or payment form. The research question is a service interaction and has explicit acknowledgement before submission. Future forms should identify purpose, required data, retention/use and the appropriate legal basis or consent mechanism; do not use a blanket checkbox for unrelated processing.

## Accessibility remaining work
Source-level hardening is present, but launch still requires automated axe/Lighthouse/WAVE checks and manual keyboard/screen-reader passes on landing, markets, screener, stock detail, methodology and legal pages. Test rendered contrast in both themes, including semantic score/verdict colors and small uppercase labels. This is not a WCAG certification.

## Open privacy/security risks
- `/status`, `/debug/jobs`, `/stocks/{symbol}/ask` and `/explain` remain unauthenticated in the local-development architecture. The project documentation already identifies them for restriction/removal before public deployment. Deployment blocker.
- Production logs need an explicit retention and access-control policy.
- `LLM_BASE_URL` is trusted configuration; production must pin it to an intended provider and document provider processing/retention terms.
- Never place credentials, payment details or sensitive personal data in client-side configuration or logs.

## Indian financial-regulatory risk
SignalDesk presents financial research, valuation, technical positioning, sentiment and AI explanations. Disclaimers do not determine regulatory status by themselves. Before paid research, personalised advice, model portfolios, explicit buy/sell/hold recommendations or other regulated services, obtain qualified Indian securities-law advice and assess SEBI registration/disclosure obligations. SEBI's current Research Analyst framework includes requirements around research services for consideration and AI-use disclosure by registered research analysts/research entities.

## Consumer/commercial risk
No paid product or checkout currently exists. Before commercial launch, replace the legal-identity notice with the real legal entity/proprietor name, business address, privacy/grievance contact, pricing, cancellation/refund terms, applicable tax details and required consumer disclosures. Do not publish invented business information.

## Copyright/assets
The current frontend uses bundled fonts, CSS/SVG graphics, application-generated charts and generated stock monograms. No remote stock-photo/logo asset or third-party iframe was identified in the repository searches performed. Any future image, logo, article excerpt, icon set, screenshot or embed must be checked for licence/permission and attribution.

## Launch blockers
1. Complete actual legal identity/contact details.
2. Obtain qualified Indian legal review before commercial launch.
3. Restrict/remove unauthenticated operational and LLM-spending endpoints before public deployment.
4. Define production log retention/access controls.
5. Confirm LLM provider processing/retention terms.
6. Run automated and manual accessibility testing across both themes.
7. Re-audit privacy/cookies/consent whenever analytics, tracking, authentication, payments or third-party embeds are introduced.
